## 💭 summary 
The research about the puzzle could be considered as the study of the line and color matching. These research tend to be inefficient if they are used for the real-world datasets. The challenge of these real-world tests are mainly from the lack of information. In term of the algorithm, a majority of the conventional methods employed the greedy algorithm. To overcome its local constraints, some global algorithms are introduced. The modern technology based on machine learning tend to extract notable image feature to reconstruct the puzzle, especially for the missing pieces or the location of the unordered pieces.
